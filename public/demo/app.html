One shift as Mads, store clerk

This is the phone app a store clerk uses. Everything is tappable. On a phone it opens full-screen, like a real app.

  1. 01Receive a pallet. Tap "Start receiving", scan the label and put it away in the bay Velo suggests.
  2. 02Trigger a sale. Tap "Simulate a checkout" (or the MK button). A refill task arrives on the phone.
  3. 03Refill the shelf. Follow the task: exact bay, batch and quantity.
  4. 04Ask the copilot on the Ask tab, or explore the store on the Map tab.
