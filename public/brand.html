01 / PaletteWhite canvas, two chromatic notes

Pure white and greys carry every screen. Exactly two chromatic notes sit on top: Signal Violet for every interactive moment and Mint Pulse for success, highlights and status dots. Tint Wash marks highlighted panels. No other accents, no gradients.

Neutrals ~70% (white, black and greys) · Violet and tint ~25% · Mint ~5%.

02 / TypePlus Jakarta Sans for headlines, Inter for UI & body

Plus Jakarta Sans SemiBold 600
Receive. Verify. Record.

Display titles, section headlines, card headings and key benchmark numbers with full English and Vietnamese glyph support.

Inter Regular 400 / Medium 500
Every delivery checked against what was ordered.

All functional UI: navigation, buttons, body copy, subtitles with generous line-height and tabular product data.

Monospace & Source

JetBrains Mono handles micro-labels, data readouts and code cards. Typography is served via Google Fonts in /tokens.css with system sans/mono fallbacks.

03 / Discipline
  • Primary actions are violet pills with white text. Secondary actions are white pills with a Mist border. Selected and active states use violet and tint; mint is never a button fill.
  • Cards, images and nav use a 16px radius; inputs 12px; pills are for buttons and badges only.
  • Flat surfaces with 1px hairlines. The only shadow is the floating nav and device mockups. No gradients, no glass.
  • Alert and success colours appear in product status only, never as decoration or a call to action.
