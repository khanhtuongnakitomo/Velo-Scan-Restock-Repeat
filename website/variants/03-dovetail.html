01 — The product

Velo

One assistant across the four zones of every store. The clerk scans a standard 1D barcode; Velo does the searching, sorting and remembering. The human always has final say.

Zone A → B

Assisted receiving & put-away

Scan the pallet, get a suggested bay, scan the rack to confirm. Batch expiry logged in one tap.

Zone D → B → C

Checkout-to-refill loop

A POS sale drops shelf stock below threshold and the clerk's phone gets one clear task.

Zone C

Local RAG copilot

Stock locations, curing times, TEK17 fire rules — answered in under two seconds, on-premise.

Network

Store-first, network-next

Every accurate store becomes a node. Cross-store transfers drafted for one-click approval.

02 — Approach

One store, then the map

Nobody re-engineers 300 stores on day one. Prove clarity inside a single store, then let the network grow out of it.

01

Empower

Months 1–3 · Denmark & Norway pilots. Barcode receiving, FEFO rotation, refill loop. Waste down 60%, shelves 99% stocked.

02

Connect

Months 4–6 · Regional goods network. Store-to-store transfers when a backroom runs dry — one-click approval.

03

Deliver

Months 6–12 · Sweden & fulfilment. Trucks dispatch only after a scanned pick confirmation at the dock.
