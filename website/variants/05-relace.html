01 / The product

One assistant. Four zones. Zero guesswork.

The clerk scans a standard 1D barcode; Velo does the searching, sorting and remembering. The human always has final say.

Zone A → B

Assisted receiving & put-away

Scan the pallet, get a suggested bay, scan the rack to confirm. Batch expiry logged in one tap — no paper checklists at the dock.

Zone D → B → C

Checkout-to-refill loop

A POS sale drops shelf stock below threshold and the clerk's phone gets one clear task: oldest batch first, exact bay, exact quantity.

Zone C

Local RAG copilot

Stock locations, curing times, TEK17 fire rules — answered in under two seconds from on-premise models. Nothing sent to any cloud.

Network

Store-first, network-next

Every accurate store becomes a node. When a bay runs dry, the assistant drafts a cross-store transfer for one-click manager approval.

One store, then the map.

Nobody re-engineers 300 stores on day one. We prove operational clarity inside a single store — then let the network grow out of it. Empower (months 1–3, DK + NO pilots), Connect (months 4–6, regional goods network), Deliver (months 6–12, Sweden fulfilment with scanned pick confirmation).

04 / Questions

Fair questions, straight answers.

Why start with the store clerk, not the delivery fleet?

Because every downstream system — transfers, fulfilment, delivery — consumes inventory truth created at the shelf and the bay. Fix the source first and the network scales itself; do it later and you automate chaos.

Is local AI actually fast enough?

Quantized 3B–7B models on a single on-premise GPU answer grounded questions in under two seconds via RAG. The workflow loop (scans, thresholds, tasks) doesn't even touch the LLM — it's deterministic logic, so the store keeps running even if the GPU naps.

What about the legacy infrared scanners?

Retired. A ruggedized smartphone with camera scanning (EAN-13 / Code-128 at <150ms) replaces $1,500–$2,200 terminals plus 20% annual maintenance contracts — a >75% cut in hardware spend.

How do three markets stay different?

One core, local configuration. Denmark starts with paperless receiving, Norway with standardized zone workflows, Sweden connects fulfilment last. Same platform, three rollout clocks.
