// 01 — THE PRODUCT & THE ROLLOUT

One assistant. Four zones. Zero guesswork.

The clerk scans a standard 1D barcode; Velo does the searching, sorting and remembering. The human always has final say.

ZONE A→B

Assisted receiving & put-away

Scan the pallet, get a suggested bay, scan the rack to confirm. Batch expiry logged in one tap.

ZONE D→C

Checkout-to-refill loop

A POS sale drops shelf stock below threshold; the clerk's phone gets one clear task.

ZONE C

Local RAG copilot

Stock, curing times, TEK17 fire rules — answered in under two seconds, on-premise.

NETWORK

Store-first, network-next

Cross-store transfers drafted automatically — one-click manager approval.
