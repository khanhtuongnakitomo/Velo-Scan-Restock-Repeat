## Speaker notes (≈ 60 giây)
> Here is how receiving works today in most small and mid-sized warehouses we looked at. A truck arrives with a paper delivery note. The employee checks it while the driver is waiting — often without the purchase order in hand. They tick a checklist. If cartons are damaged, it is written by hand, or forgotten. Hours or days later, someone re-types it into Excel. And from that moment, the system says the goods are in stock — whether they are or not.
> A tick proves a form was filled in. It does not prove the quantity, the product, the condition, the batch, or who checked it.

## Lưu ý
- Câu "most warehouses" chỉ dùng khi đã có phỏng vấn. Chưa có → nói "the warehouses we observed".
- Không đổ lỗi cho nhân viên: vấn đề là **thiết kế quy trình**.
