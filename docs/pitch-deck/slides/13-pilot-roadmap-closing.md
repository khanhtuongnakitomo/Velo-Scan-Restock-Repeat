## Speaker notes (≈ 60 giây)
> We do not ask anyone to believe our numbers. We ask for one warehouse and eight weeks. Two weeks to prepare. Two weeks running VELO next to paper, so nothing is at risk. Four weeks live. At the end, real numbers go into our value model — and the owner decides.
> Verified receiving is only the start: once stock is true, we can guide put-away, rotate expiring batches and predict demand — each step possible only because the previous one made the data real.
> Receive. Verify. Record. Zero stock by assumption. Thank you.
