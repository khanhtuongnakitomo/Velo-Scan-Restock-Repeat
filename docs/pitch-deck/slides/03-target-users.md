## Headline
`BUILT FOR VIETNAM'S BUILDING-MATERIALS DISTRIBUTORS FIRST.`

## Nội dung trên slide

- **Kicker:** `03 · TARGET BUSINESS & USERS`
- **Card khách hàng đầu tiên (beachhead):**
  ```
  BEACHHEAD CUSTOMER
  Building-materials & industrial-supplies distributors
  · 1–5 warehouses · hundreds to thousands of SKUs
  · supplier deliveries every day
  · stock run on paper, Excel and Zalo today

  WHY START HERE
  · bulky goods → frequent transit damage
  · cement, sealants, paint → batch & expiry risk
  · mixed-SKU trucks → partial deliveries, substitutions
  ```
- **4 persona card:**

| Persona | Pain (1 dòng) | VELO gives them |
|---|---|---|
| `RECEIVER` | `Counts, inspects and does paperwork at once — blamed when stock goes missing` | `A guided check and a record that protects them` |
| `WAREHOUSE MANAGER` | `Hears about damage hours later, in chat, with no evidence` | `An exception queue with photos and one-tap approval` |
| `OWNER / FINANCE` | `Pays invoices for goods nobody verified` | `Receipts that prove what was accepted` |
| `DRIVER` (participant) | `Disputes argued from memory` | `Co-confirms discrepancies on the spot` |

- **Dải dưới:** `Next: any SME distribution warehouse — FMCG, electrical, spare parts. Same receiving problem.`

## Speaker notes (≈ 50 giây)
> We start narrow on purpose: building-materials and industrial-supplies distributors with one to five warehouses. Their goods are heavy and get damaged in transit; cement and sealants expire; one truck carries many products. That is where verification pays most.
> Four people live with this problem. The receiver does everything at once and gets blamed later. The manager learns about damage hours late. The owner pays for goods nobody verified. And the driver argues from memory. VELO gives each of them something concrete.

## Lưu ý
- Nếu phỏng vấn cho thấy phân khúc khác đau hơn, đổi beachhead — đừng giữ vì đã viết sẵn.
- Không đưa quy mô thị trường (TAM) nếu không có nguồn số liệu rõ ràng.
