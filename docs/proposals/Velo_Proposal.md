## 5. Our Solution: Velo (Human-Centric Workflow Assistant & 1D Barcode Platform)

**Velo** is a zone-based **Store & Warehouse Operations Assistant** that combines **Smartphone 1D Barcode Scanning**, an **AI Workflow Organizer**, and a **Sovereign Local RAG Copilot**.

### 5.1 Strategic Hardware Consulting: Replacing Legacy Infrared Scanners with a Smart Mobile Assistant
*(Note: This directly shows the judges we understand physical store operations, hardware maintenance costs, and employee ergonomics!)*

Traditional construction warehouses rely on legacy dedicated infrared handhelds (e.g., aging Windows CE / proprietary gun terminals).
* **Why Legacy Infrared Scanners Hold Employees Back:**
  1. **High Cost & Constant Maintenance:** Each device costs **$\$1,500\text{--}\$2,200$** plus expensive repair contracts when laser modules, physical triggers, or proprietary charging cradles fail.
  2. **Zero Workflow Intelligence ("Dumb Beepers"):** Legacy infrared terminals only beep and show a raw SKU code on a tiny screen. They **cannot show the clerk where an item is located, which batch expires first, or how to prioritize their next task**.
* **Why Our Smartphone App Can Completely Replace Legacy Scanners:**
  * **Real-World Division of Labor at the Dock:** In a construction store, while heavy equipment operators move pallets off the truck, an **assisting Store Clerk** verifies and checks in the delivery. Because the clerk's role is verification and organization rather than heavy lifting, they can comfortably use the **Velo Mobile App** on a standard ruggedized smartphone.
  * **Fast 1D Barcode Camera Scanning ($<150\text{ms}$):** Using modern mobile camera vision optimized for `EAN-13` (products) and `Code-128` (warehouse bay labels), the clerk scans barcodes just as fast as a handheld gun.
  * **Upgrading From a "Scanner" to a "Personal Assistant":** Instead of carrying a dumb laser gun, every clerk now carries an interactive visual guide in their pocket that maps warehouse bays, highlights expiring stock, organizes refill priorities, and answers technical questions—while cutting Company A’s scanner hardware & maintenance budget by **$>75\%$**.

### Feature 1: Assisted Receiving, 1D Barcode Zone Mapping & Expiry/Ageing Organizer
* **Store Zones Covered:** **Zone A (Receiving Dock)** $\rightarrow$ **Zone B (Back Warehouse & Yard)**.
* **Solves:** Manual Processes (Denmark) & Disorganized Backroom Storage (Norway).
* **How the Workflow Assists the Clerk (Step-by-Step):**
  1. **Scan Product 1D Barcode During Unloading:** As pallets are unloaded at the receiving dock, the assisting store clerk scans the product's standard **1D SKU Barcode (`EAN-13`)** using the Velo smartphone app, eliminating paper delivery checklists.
  2. **AI-Guided Put-Away & Warehouse Bay Barcode Scan:** Instead of the clerk wandering the back warehouse looking for space, the AI Assistant recommends an optimal bay based on item turnover and category: *"Suggested Put-Away: Front-Accessible Bay A-02 (High-Demand Item)."* Once placed, the clerk scans the **1D Area/Bay Barcode Sticker (`Code-128`)** on the rack beam (e.g., `ZONE-WH-BAY-04`) to confirm. If `Bay A-02` is blocked, the clerk simply scans whichever bay they choose—**the human always has final say**, and the system updates the map instantly.
  3. **Due-Date, Old Stock & Out-of-Stock Check:** During receiving (or quick periodic zone checks), the clerk logs the batch expiration date with one tap (critical for cement, adhesives, paint, and sealants).
* **How the AI Assistant Organizes the Clerk’s Workflow (Without Replacing Them):**
  * Rather than acting like a rigid boss, the **Velo Workflow Organizer** acts like a helpful **personal planner** for the store team:
    * **Clear Daily Priority Checklist:** Every morning, the AI reviews batch due dates and stock levels, presenting the clerk with a clean, stress-free **"Morning Action Guide"** sorted by urgency.
    * **FEFO (First-Expired, First-Out) Guidance:** When a batch of *SikaFlex Sealant* in `Bay B-04` is within 14 days of its due date (or has been sitting unsold for $>90\text{ days}$), the AI highlights it on the clerk's screen so they know to bring that older batch to the front shelf first before opening a new pallet—preventing waste effortlessly.

### Feature 2: Event-Driven Checkout-to-Refill Loop (Closed-Loop Shelf Assistant)
* **Store Zones Covered:** **Zone D (Customer POS Checkout)** $\rightarrow$ **Zone B (Back Warehouse)** $\rightarrow$ **Zone C (Front Display Shelf)**.
* **Solves:** Inconsistent Store Operations (Norway) & On-Shelf Out-of-Stocks.
* **How It Makes the Clerk Faster & More Organized:** Instead of forcing clerks to walk up and down every aisle all day checking if shelves are empty, Velo tracks POS checkout events in real time and notifies the clerk **only when and where a refill is actually needed**:
  1. **Customer Buys Item at Checkout:** A contractor buys 15 boxes of decking screws at the POS checkout (leaving only 3 boxes on the front shelf in `Aisle 2`, which is below the minimum threshold of 10).
  2. **System Acknowledges Checkout Event:** The POS webhook sends the purchase event to Velo. The system deducts 15 boxes from `Front Shelf - Aisle 2` and detects that leftover front-shelf stock is low ($3 < 10 \rightarrow \text{REFILL NEEDED}$).
  3. **AI Assistant Prepares a Clear Refill Guide for the Clerk:** The AI checks the Back Warehouse map, locates 80 boxes in `Back Warehouse - Bay C-03`, identifies which batch is oldest (FEFO), and adds a clear **Refill Card** to the store clerk's mobile task list:
     > 🔔 **ASSISTED REFILL TASK:** *"Front Shelf Aisle 2 is low on Decking Screws ($3\text{ left}$ after recent checkout). **Suggested Action:** Pick **20 boxes** from **Back Warehouse Bay C-03 (Batch #401 - Oldest Stock)** and refill **Front Shelf Aisle 2**."*
  4. **1D Barcode Scan to Confirm & Continue Loop:** The clerk walks straight to `Bay C-03` (zero searching!), scans the bay's 1D barcode + product's 1D barcode to confirm the pick, restocks `Aisle 2`, and taps "Complete." If back-warehouse stock drops below safety levels after this refill, the AI prepares a draft restock request for the Store Manager to review and approve with one click.

## 6. Cybersecurity & European Sovereignty: Why We Use 100% Local AI

A critical pillar of **Netcompany’s corporate DNA** (highlighted on Slide 5 & 6 of the Case Study PDF) is *"Building European sovereignty for the age of AI — secure, reliable, and transparent digital solutions."*

Sending Company A’s sensitive B2B pricing, inventory levels, supplier delivery schedules, and internal store workflows to external public cloud LLM APIs exposes the company to **API hijacking, man-in-the-middle data interception, prompt-injection leaks, and GDPR/corporate espionage risks**.

### How Velo Guarantees Security & Responsible AI:
1. **Self-Hosted Open-Weight LLMs (Zero External Data Leakage):**
   * We run **Local AI inference** using **Ollama / vLLM** hosting quantized open-weight models (**Llama 3.2 / Qwen 2.5 / Mistral**) and local embedding models (**BGE-M3 / Nomic-Embed-Text**) directly inside Company A’s private on-premise server or sovereign European VPC.
2. **Immunity to External API Hijacking & Cloud Outages:**
   * Because all AI reasoning, RAG retrieval, and workflow organization run on the local network, **hackers cannot hijack external API keys or intercept proprietary retail payloads over the public internet**. Even if external internet connectivity is disrupted, the store’s local AI and warehouse barcode workflows continue running smoothly.
3. **Human-in-the-Loop Guardrails (Employees Always in Control):**
   * Velo never alters inventory counts, disposes of expiring goods, or places external orders behind the scenes without human confirmation. The AI **organizes, recommends, and alerts**—while the store clerk or manager confirms every action via a simple barcode scan or button tap.
